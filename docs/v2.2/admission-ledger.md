- **Nothing was executed.** No test, fixture, validator, linter, installer, release check, live probe or network verification was run for this record. Every source below is labelled *inspected* or *installed*, never *verified* or *executed*.
- **Text-only admission.** This record admits sources, decisions, holds and destinations into the record. It admits no runtime behavior, installs nothing, changes no tracker item, and grants no authority.
- **Policy defaults are not proposed.** `completion-result-v1` is **settled in the SDP** by D4.5 (l.426–l.450; §14 l.810); the five qualifications C1–C5 of the Architect response (§1 A25) are answered within it. This ledger proposes no value, schema, field, threshold or default beyond what the SDP records, and re-asks nothing.

AC-07 becomes a U1 adoption gate rather than a V2-R gate (l.755). The three panel critiques stay open and still gate the panel technical plan (§3.3); no panel scope, acceptance, hold or critique is relaxed here.
- **Tracker states are point-in-time.** Compact tracker summaries (number, title, state, milestone, labels) were read on 2026-11-04. Deep reads were limited to `#183`, `#132` and `#182`. Live tracker state wins over any statement here.
- **Milestone and issue IDs in the SDP are planning labels.** The SDP states that its planning milestone IDs do not create tracker milestones or assign work to live agents — "Planning milestone IDs here do not create tracker milestones" (SDP l.13). The M1–M6 milestones referenced below are real tracker milestones (`#8`–`#13`); the P0–P5 phase labels are planning labels.
